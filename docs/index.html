Privacy Policy — Turru: Stealth Chat & Call Shield

Effective Date: 20 November 2026 • Developer: 311devlabs

This Privacy Policy explains how Turru: Stealth Chat & Call Shield ("the App", package com.turru.stealthshield) accesses, processes, and protects data on your Android device.

1. Local-First Privacy Architecture & Opt-In Hashed Community Protection

  • All incoming messages, notification media, device contacts, Allow/Block rules, and personal call logs are stored 100% locally in an on-device SQLite database (android:allowBackup="false") and are NEVER uploaded, sold, or shared with any server or third party.
  • By default, the Community Spam Database feature is OFF, meaning the App operates completely offline with zero network traffic.
  • Internet access (INTERNET) is used strictly on an opt-in basis only if you explicitly enable the Community Spam Database to check or report unknown spam callers—transmitting solely salted one-way SHA-256 hashes of unknown numbers, structured category tags, and an anonymous app-generated reporter ID over HTTPS.

2. Sensitive Permissions Disclosure

A. Notification Access (android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE)

  • Data Accessed: Text and image previews of incoming message notifications and incoming call notification status from supported apps (WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, and SMS).
  • Purpose of Use:
    • Displaying incoming messages locally inside the App so you can read them privately without triggering read receipts, and preserving messages deleted by the sender (Anti-Delete).
    • Detecting incoming VoIP call notifications from unknown numbers, displaying Caller ID context, and automatically declining blocked calls (PendingIntent Decline) according to your rules.
  • Storage: Stored exclusively on your device's internal storage and never transmitted off-device.

B. Contacts Permission (android.permission.READ_CONTACTS)

  • Data Accessed: Contact display names and phone numbers saved in your device's address book.
  • Purpose of Use:
    • Verifying locally whether an incoming caller is already a saved contact so friends and family are never blocked or queried against the Community Spam server.
    • Helping you pick phone numbers directly from your contacts to add to your Allow List (Whitelist) or Block List (Blacklist).
  • Storage: Read strictly on-device during call matching or contact selection; your contacts are never uploaded or shared.

C. Biometric Authentication (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC)

  • Data Accessed: Local authentication confirmation via the Android system BiometricPrompt API (fingerprint/face unlock), when enabled by you in Settings.
  • Storage: Biometric credentials are handled entirely by your Android operating system hardware security module. The App never accesses, stores, or transmits raw biometric data.

D. Cellular Call Screening, Caller ID Overlay & Notifications (BIND_SCREENING_SERVICE / ROLE_CALL_SCREENING, SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW, POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

  • Data Accessed: Incoming cellular (GSM) phone number provided by Android's official CallScreeningService API, only if you voluntarily choose Turru as your Android Caller ID & Spam app.
  • Purpose of Use:
    • Identifying unknown cellular callers locally (carrier, city/country prefix, public official hotlines, cross-reference with local chat history in Turru, and your personal tags) and optionally checking hashed spam reputation if Community Spam is enabled.
    • Automatically rejecting calls from spam-tagged or unknown numbers according to your rules.
    • Displaying a real-time floating Caller ID card (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW, optional) or heads-up notification (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) while your phone rings.
  • Compliance: Uses Android's official ROLE_CALL_SCREENING role and does not request restricted READ_CALL_LOG or READ_PHONE_STATE permissions.

E. Optional Community Spam Database (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Opt-In Only)

  • Opt-In Control: Disabled by default. No data is transmitted unless you explicitly enable Community Spam Database after consenting to the in-app disclosure.
  • Data Transmitted When Enabled (HTTPS TLS Encrypted):
    • Salted one-way SHA-256 hash of an unknown caller's normalized phone number (raw phone numbers are never stored on the server; saved Contacts are never queried).
    • Structured spam/safe category tag (e.g. Scam, Debt Collector, Telemarketing, Courier, Safe) and optional sanitized short label (only displayed publicly if corroborated by at least 2 distinct devices).
    • Anonymous random reporter_id (a hashed random UUID stored locally; not linked to your name, email, Google account, IMEI, or phone number) used solely for 1-device-1-vote anti-abuse and daily rate-limiting.
  • No Third-Party Sharing or Ads: Data is processed solely by our dedicated Cloudflare Workers & D1 endpoint (https://turru-spam-api.digiwork-jkt.workers.dev) for fraud/spam prevention and is never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

3. User Control & Data Deletion

  • You may delete individual chats, all saved messages, call logs, personal tags, or the offline community spam cache at any time inside the App.
  • You may turn off Community Spam Database or revoke Notification Access, Contacts, Caller ID & Spam Role, or Overlay permissions at any time in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently removes the entire local SQLite database and anonymous reporter_id from your device. Community reports contain only anonymous one-way hashes with no personally identifiable information.
  • For privacy or deletion inquiries, contact us at support@turru.site.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

Kebijakan Privasi — Turru: Stealth Chat & Call Shield

Tanggal Efektif: 20 November 2026 • Pengembang: 311devlabs

Kebijakan Privasi ini menjelaskan bagaimana aplikasi Turru: Stealth Chat & Call Shield ("Aplikasi", ID paket com.turru.stealthshield) mengakses, memproses, dan melindungi data di perangkat Android Anda.

1. Prinsip Privasi Utama (Local-First) & Perlindungan Komunitas Opt-In Berbasis Hash

  • Seluruh isi pesan masuk, media pratinjau notifikasi, Buku Kontak HP, daftar Allow/Blokir, dan riwayat panggilan pribadi disimpan 100% secara lokal di database SQLite internal perangkat Anda (android:allowBackup="false") dan TIDAK pernah diunggah, dijual, atau dibagikan ke server atau pihak ketiga mana pun.
  • Secara bawaan (default), fitur Database Spam Komunitas dalam keadaan NONAKTIF, sehingga Aplikasi bekerja sepenuhnya secara offline tanpa lalu lintas jaringan.
  • Akses internet (INTERNET) hanya digunakan secara sukarela (opt-in) jika Anda mengaktifkan Database Spam Komunitas untuk mengecek atau melaporkan nomor spam—dan data yang dikirim melalui HTTPS hanyalah hash satu arah SHA-256 dari nomor asing, kategori tag terstruktur, serta ID pelapor acak anonim.

2. Pengungkapan Penggunaan Izin Sensitif (Prominent Disclosure)

A. Izin Akses Notifikasi (android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE)

  • Data yang Diakses: Teks dan pratinjau gambar notifikasi pesan masuk serta status notifikasi panggilan masuk dari aplikasi yang didukung (WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, dan SMS).
  • Tujuan Penggunaan:
    • Menampilkan isi pesan masuk di dalam Aplikasi agar dapat dibaca tanpa memicu tanda baca (Mode Siluman) serta menyimpan pesan yang dihapus oleh pengirim (Anti-Delete).
    • Mendeteksi panggilan VoIP masuk dari nomor tidak dikenal, menampilkan info Caller ID, dan menolak otomatis panggilan sesuai aturan Daftar Allow/Blokir Anda (PendingIntent Decline).
  • Penyimpanan: Disimpan hanya di memori internal perangkat Anda dan tidak pernah dikirim keluar perangkat.

B. Izin Membaca Kontak (android.permission.READ_CONTACTS)

  • Data yang Diakses: Nama tampilan kontak dan nomor telepon yang tersimpan di Buku Kontak perangkat Anda.
  • Tujuan Penggunaan:
    • Memverifikasi secara lokal apakah penelepon merupakan kontak tersimpan agar panggilan teman/keluarga tidak salah diblokir dan tidak pernah dicek ke server komunitas.
    • Memudahkan Anda memilih nomor dari buku telepon untuk dimasukkan ke Daftar Allow (Whitelist) atau Daftar Blokir (Blacklist).
  • Penyimpanan: Hanya dibaca secara lokal di perangkat Anda; daftar kontak Anda tidak pernah dikirim atau dibagikan ke server mana pun.

C. Autentikasi Biometrik (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC)

  • Data yang Diakses: Konfirmasi autentikasi lokal melalui sistem Android BiometricPrompt (sidik jari / pengenalan wajah) apabila Anda mengaktifkan fitur Kunci Aplikasi.
  • Penyimpanan: Proses biometrik ditangani sepenuhnya oleh perangkat keras keamanan Android. Aplikasi tidak pernah membaca, menyimpan, maupun mengirim data biometrik Anda.

D. Penyaringan Panggilan Seluler, Overlay Caller ID & Notifikasi (BIND_SCREENING_SERVICE / ROLE_CALL_SCREENING, SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW, POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

  • Data yang Diakses: Nomor telepon seluler (GSM) masuk melalui API resmi CallScreeningService Android, hanya jika Anda memilih Turru sebagai aplikasi ID Penelepon & Spam bawaan Android.
  • Tujuan Penggunaan:
    • Mengidentifikasi nomor asing secara lokal (operator seluler, kode wilayah/negara, direktori nomor resmi publik, kecocokan riwayat chat lokal di Turru, dan tag pribadi Anda) serta mengecek reputasi hash spam jika fitur Komunitas diaktifkan.
    • Menolak otomatis panggilan dari nomor bertag spam atau nomor tidak dikenal sesuai aturan Anda.
    • Menampilkan kartu popup Caller ID di atas layar (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW, opsional) atau notifikasi heads-up (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) saat ponsel berdering.
  • Kepatuhan: Menggunakan peran resmi ROLE_CALL_SCREENING Android dan tidak meminta izin terbatas READ_CALL_LOG atau READ_PHONE_STATE.

E. Database Spam Komunitas Opsional (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Khusus Opt-In)

  • Kendali Opt-In: Nonaktif secara bawaan. Tidak ada koneksi internet selama fitur ini tidak Anda aktifkan.
  • Data yang Dikirim Jika Diaktifkan (Terenkripsi HTTPS TLS):
    • Hash satu arah SHA-256 (dengan salt) dari nomor telepon asing (server tidak menyimpan nomor mentah; nomor di Buku Kontak tidak pernah dicek).
    • Kategori tag terstruktur (mis. Penipuan, Pinjol/DC, Telemarketing, Kurir, Aman) dan label singkat opsional yang hanya tampil ke publik jika dikonfirmasi minimal 2 perangkat berbeda.
    • ID pelapor acak anonim (reporter_id) berupa hash UUID lokal (bukan IMEI, bukan akun Google, bukan nomor HP Anda) semata-mata untuk aturan 1 perangkat = 1 suara dan pembatasan spam laporan.
  • Tanpa Iklan & Tanpa Penjualan Data: Data diproses khusus oleh server Cloudflare Workers & D1 milik kami (https://turru-spam-api.digiwork-jkt.workers.dev) untuk pencegahan spam/penipuan dan tidak pernah dijual atau dibagikan ke pengiklan atau broker data.

3. Kontrol Pengguna & Penghapusan Data

  • Anda dapat menghapus riwayat percakapan, seluruh pesan tersimpan, log panggilan, tag nomor pribadi, maupun cache spam komunitas kapan saja di dalam Aplikasi.
  • Anda dapat mematikan Database Spam Komunitas atau mencabut izin Akses Notifikasi, Kontak, peran Caller ID & Spam, maupun Overlay kapan saja lewat Pengaturan.
  • Menghapus instalasi (uninstall) Aplikasi akan menghapus permanen seluruh database SQLite lokal dan reporter_id anonim dari perangkat Anda.
  • Untuk pertanyaan privasi atau permintaan penghapusan data, hubungi support@turru.site.
